Method
------
xcrun simctl io <udid> recordVideo writes a VARIABLE-frame-rate H.264 file: one frame is
emitted per composited screen update. Frame count over a fixed wall-clock window is
therefore a direct read-out of how often the app rendered.

  xcrun simctl terminate <udid> com.agentcy.app
  xcrun simctl launch <udid> com.agentcy.app -agentCyPreviewData -agentCyPreviewTab <tab>
  sleep 12                          # let the launch settle
  xcrun simctl io <udid> recordVideo --codec h264 --force idle_<tab>.mov &
  sleep 10                          # NO INPUT AT ALL during this window
  <stop recorder>
  ffprobe -v error -select_streams v:0 -show_entries stream=nb_frames,duration idle_<tab>.mov
