Method
------
The app already emits os_log notices from RootLaunchDiagnostics (App/RootView.swift:43-86)
with an elapsed_ms since process init. No instrumentation was added.

  xcrun simctl spawn <udid> log stream --style compact \
    --predicate 'subsystem == "com.agentcy.app"' > launch.log &
  xcrun simctl launch <udid> com.agentcy.app <fixture flags>

`destination_app` is emitted from RootView's .onAppear, i.e. after SwiftUI has built the
whole tree for the first frame. Script: scratchpad/l2/launchtime.sh

Two readings
------------
1. Home-selected and Plan-selected launches cost the same (mean 553.5 ms vs 532.5 ms) even
   though HomeDashboardView declares 12 whole-table @Query and 29 derived array properties
   while PlanView declares 8 and 0. The shell builds all six tab roots regardless of which
   one is selected — AppShellView.swift:62-83 puts six NavigationStacks in one ZStack and
   only sets opacity(0) on the unselected ones (appTabLayer, AppShellView.swift:733-741).

2. ResumablePostEditorView alone costs 234–275 ms of main-thread build. That work has to
   finish before iOS can begin a sheet presentation, which is what "sheets feel slow"
   measures.

Note on Instruments
-------------------
`xcrun xctrace record --template 'Time Profiler' --device <sim udid> --attach <pid>
--time-limit 30s` was attempted twice. Both runs created the .trace directory and then hung
with 52 KB written; killed after 5 minutes. No trace was produced, so no top-frames table
is available. Every number above comes from the app's own os_log milestones and from
variable-frame-rate screen recordings (see idle-frames-by-tab.txt).
